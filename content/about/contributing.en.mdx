## Submission and contact

- **Discord:** Find `douobb` on the official CyberCode Online Discord and send a direct message. Text and files are accepted.
- **In game:** Submitters can message `douobb` in game. In-game messages cannot include attachments, so send files through Discord.

## Submission format

Submitters can paste plain text directly into a Discord or in-game message. To attach a text submission as a file, use UTF-8 encoded `.md` (Markdown, preferred) or `.txt` (plain text). Use a clear title, organize sections with `##`, and list key points. Name files by topic and language, for example `search-reward-correction-en.md`.

Please include the relevant page or section, language, suggested correction or complete draft, and sources for any figures or formulas. Specify the preferred attribution; the default is the submitter's in-game player name. Fields that do not apply can be omitted. The site owner will handle layout and format conversion; submitters only need to organize the text.

The following template can be used, with fields that do not apply left out:

```md
# [Submission topic]

- Related page or section:
- Language:
- Preferred attribution:

## Suggested correction or complete draft

[Describe the suggestion or paste the complete draft]

## Sources for figures or formulas

[Source or link]
```

## What you can contribute

- **Article corrections or additions:** Identify the page and section, then explain the change and why it is needed. Include a verifiable source for game values.
- **Complete guides:** Provide steps, use cases, necessary details, and sources.
- **Tool bug reports:** Include reproduction steps, expected and actual results, and the operating environment.
- **Tool or article suggestions:** Describe the use case, the problem to solve, and the expected improvement.
- **Translations:** Preserve the original meaning and structure, use the correct in-game terminology, and proofread the result. Do not submit unchecked machine translations.

Submissions should be relevant to this site, complete, and verifiable. AI assistance is permitted, but submitters remain responsible for checking and proofreading their work.

## Rights, review, and adoption

Submitters must confirm that they created the submitted work or have sufficient rights to let this site use it. For third-party sources, images, or screenshots, include the source and license information. Material without the necessary rights or permission must not be submitted.

Reviews consider accuracy, completeness, sources, and whether the submission fits the site. We may ask for more information, combine duplicate submissions, or decline work that is unrelated, unverifiable, or low quality. Repeated low-quality submissions or abusive behavior will first receive a warning. Continued abuse after repeated warnings may result in blocked submission privileges and removal from the public contributor list. Attribution for published work is handled separately; deliberate plagiarism or fabrication may result in an immediate block.

Before adopting a submission, the site owner will contact the submitter through one of the channels above and obtain explicit consent: original articles or guides will be published under CC BY-SA 4.0, and code contributions under the MIT License. The preferred public attribution will be confirmed separately. A submission or commit message does not itself grant a license. If explicit consent is not obtained, or the submitter declines the applicable license, the material will not be published. Submitters may only grant permission for work they created or have the right to license; third-party material without the necessary rights or permission will not be included.
