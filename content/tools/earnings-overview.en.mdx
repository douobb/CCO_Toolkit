## Usage and output

Enter the scavenging, molecular printing, and mining levels, then adjust bargain, the BTC buff, and the comparison mode. “Fill from shared settings” imports shared levels and bargain while preserving this tool's buff setting.

The results table compares net earnings across activities. Per-minute mode shows unit-time earnings, while time-skip mode compares the total from batches that fit within 15, 30, 45, 60, or 105 minutes.

“Earnings by level” uses variable- and fixed-earnings charts to show level differences. Use the legend to toggle activities and inspect a selected level; on narrow screens, the chart scrolls horizontally.
